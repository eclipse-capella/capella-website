When the Arcadia Method Meets SysML v2

Capella for SysON brings the concepts and modeling approach of Arcadia to SysON, an open-source, web-based modeling environment built for SysML v2.

Designed as an extesnion to SysON, it extends this SysML v2 native tool with capabilities dedicated to Arcadia.

At its core, the project relies on a SysML v2 library for Arcadia, extending SysML v2 concepts, so that systems can be modeled according to the Arcadia method. By extending SysML v2 with Arcadia concepts and introducing Capella-inspired graphical representations, the project aims to combine the benefits of a standard modeling language with the methodological guidance of Arcadia.

This approach enables engineers to work with SysML v2 models while following the structured engineering activities they already know from Capella.

 

Building the Future of Capella, Step by Step

Developed by Obeo, in collaboration with Thales, Capella for SysON builds on three complementary open-source components:

  • Arcadia SysML v2 Library: Formalizes Arcadia concepts using SysML v2.
  • Capella SysML v2 Bridge: Enables the transformation of existing Capella models into SysML v2.
  • Capella-inspired tooling: Provides Arcadia-specific modeling capabilities and Capella-inspired graphical representations within SysON.

Project Roadmap

  1. 2026. Open-Source Foundations
    • Development of the Arcadia library, the Capella-to-SysML v2 bridge, and the first Capella-inspired modeling capabilities in SysON.
  2. 2027. First MVP
    • A first integrated environment for evaluating Capella for SysON on concrete Arcadia use cases and gathering feedback.
  3. Beyond 2027. Progressive Expansion
    • Broader Arcadia coverage, additional Capella capabilities, improved interoperability, and migration support for existing Capella projects.

What about Capella Desktop?

Capella Desktop remains the mature solution for operational Arcadia projects. Capella for SysON is a progressive initiative, designed to provide a future evolution path without disrupting existing engineering investments.
